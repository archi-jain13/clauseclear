    ClauseClear Pipeline Architecture

    End-to-end design specifications, chunking heuristics, and anti-hallucination validation mechanisms.

    1

    Legal Clause Chunking

    Unlike naive token windows that split clauses midway through a sentence, ClauseClear segments documents along legal structural boundaries (Section headers, Article numbers, Roman numerals) to preserve full legal context.

    2

    Vector Standard Retrieval

    Each clause is embedded and matched against a curated vector database of standard baseline clauses, using LanceDB with sentence-transformers MiniLM embeddings and a TF-IDF fallback. This gives the model a concrete reference to compare against rather than relying on ungrounded intuition.

    3

    Stage 2 Validation Pass

    High-risk flags trigger a self-consistency check asking: "Is this genuinely predatory or standard legalese unfamiliar to laypeople?" This eliminates false alarms on standard boilerplate like severability or joint liability clauses.
